My Creative Story

My interest in design started through music visuals, cover art, and digital graphics. Over time, I became more interested in how design can shape a complete experience, from a single image to an entire website.

In school, I am learning how to combine HTML, CSS, accessibility, responsive design, and layout systems into modern web experiences. I enjoy creating work that feels organized, clean, and visually strong.

Outside of class, I create graphics and promotional content for NOVA Campus Recreation. Working on real projects has helped me understand how design can improve communication and audience engagement.

How I Work

Step 1
Understand the idea
I start by learning the purpose of the project, the audience, and the message that needs to be communicated.
Step 2
Plan the layout
I organize the content, choose a clear structure, and think about spacing, hierarchy, and flow.
Step 3
Create the design
I build the visual direction using typography, color, imagery, and layout to make the project feel clean and consistent.
Step 4
Review and improve
I test the design, check details, and make improvements based on feedback so the final work becomes stronger and clearer.
