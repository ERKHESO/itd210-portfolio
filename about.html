About Me

Designer + Creative Thinker

Hi, I’m Erkhes O, a graphic designer and creative storyteller currently studying at Northern Virginia Community College (NOVA). My work lives at the intersection of visual identity, digital design, and cultural storytelling. I’m passionate about building brands that feel meaningful, modern, and globally connected.

I specialize in branding, typography, and digital media, and I enjoy turning ideas into clear visual systems—from logos and posters to websites and interactive experiences. Many of my projects explore how design can communicate culture, emotion, and identity while still maintaining a clean, modern aesthetic.

Outside the classroom, I work with NOVA Campus Recreation, creating promotional graphics, social media content, and visual campaigns that encourage students to stay active and connected. Designing for real communities has helped me understand how design can influence engagement and create impact beyond the screen.

My goal is to continue growing as a designer, build a strong portfolio of meaningful work, and eventually collaborate with global brands and creative teams that value culture, innovation, and strong visual identity.

When I’m not designing, you’ll probably find me working out at the gym, exploring creative ideas, or developing new projects that blend design, storytelling, and culture.
